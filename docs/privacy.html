Privacy

Tempmail is a static page. It has no backend of its own, no database, and no user accounts. This document describes what the page does and does not do with data. Last updated 2026-08-28.

What stays in your browser

Your current temporary address, the provider session token, and your language and theme choice are kept in this browser's localStorage. They never leave your device through this page. Clearing site data, or pressing Burn it, removes them.

What goes to third parties

Mail is fetched directly by your browser from the disposable-mail provider you select - Mail.gw or Guerrilla Mail. Those requests carry your IP address and browser headers to that provider, and the provider stores the messages sent to your address. Their privacy policies and retention rules apply to that data, not this page's. Fonts are served from this page's own files - no third-party font request.

What this page does not do

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no cookies set by this page, and nothing is sent to a server operated by whoever runs this copy of Tempmail. There is no server to send it to.

A Content-Security-Policy limits network access to the two mail APIs only, scripts to this page's own files, and a no-referrer policy keeps the page URL out of every outbound request. Opened messages render in a sandboxed frame with a stricter policy that blocks remote images (tracking pixels), scripts and web fonts.

Notifications

Desktop notifications are optional and off by default. If you enable them, the permission is managed by your browser and can be revoked there at any time. Notification text (sender and subject) is shown only on your device.

Anonymity is limited

A temporary inbox is not full anonymity. The provider can see your IP, some sites block known disposable domains, and anyone with access to this browser session can read whatever is in the inbox. Never use a temporary address for anything private, valuable, or tied to your identity.
