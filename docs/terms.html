What it is

A browser-only client that generates a disposable email address and shows mail sent to it, by talking directly to public disposable-mail APIs. It is free, needs no signup, and is provided as-is.

Receive-only

Temporary inboxes can only receive mail. This page cannot send email and offers no way to reply.

No guarantees

There is no uptime, delivery, or retention guarantee. Addresses and messages are wiped by the upstream provider on its own schedule (Mail.gw after 7 days, Guerrilla Mail after 1 hour) and cannot be recovered. A provider may rate-limit or block requests, reject an address, or go offline at any time. Verification codes may be delayed or never arrive.
